Introducing Mobi-π: Mobilizing Your Robot Learning Policy.

Our method:

  • ✈️ enables flexible mobile skill chaining
  • 🪶 without requiring additional policy training data
  • 🏠 while scaling to unseen scenes

Jingyun Yang @yjy0625

We introduce the "policy mobilization" problem. Given an existing manipulation policy trained on data collected from limited viewpoints, policy mobilization aims to find an optimal robot pose in an unseen environment to successfully execute this policy on a mobile platform.

1/6

Jingyun Yang @yjy0625

What makes a good policy mobilization method? We propose a new method consisting of four simple steps:

  1. 1️⃣ Capture the test-time scene
  2. 2️⃣ Create a 3D Gaussian Splat
  3. 3️⃣ Compute hybrid scores of rendered views
  4. 4️⃣ Optimize!

We show in simulated and real-world experiments that our policy mobilization method is

  • 💡 more data-efficient than an end-to-end mobile manipulation policy
  • 💡 more robust than naive object navigation methods

We demonstrate that policy mobilization enables flexible deployment of locally-trained policies on a mobile platform in the real world, including:

  • ✅ scaling to large scenes
  • ✅ generalization to unseen room layouts and object positions
  • ✅ composition of atomic skills
